Timeline & measurement

03 / How the pilot runs

Prepare. Test. Learn.

The pilot runs for 45 days from kickoff, with launch dependent on list quality, approvals, and sending readiness.

Days 01–10

Prepare

Review the setup, agree the audience, check a sample list, and approve the first messages.

Days 11–30

Test

Launch at a safe pace, review human replies, and check whether the right buyers are responding.

Days 31–45

Improve

Refine the offer and targeting, document the results, and agree what should happen next.

If access, approvals, or inbox readiness cause delays, agree a revised schedule before sending.

What we’ll measure

MeasureWhat we’ll review
List qualityCompany fit, role fit, current employment, exclusions, and email verification are separate checks.
Human repliesUnique human respondents divided by unique people reached. Exclude automatic replies.
Buyer interestInterested respondents divided by human respondents, with counts and examples alongside the percentage.
Sales progressTrack qualified conversations and next steps. Revenue can follow after the pilot ends.
What success means

Use list quality, relevant replies, and sales conversations to judge the test. No reply rate, meeting count, or revenue outcome is guaranteed.
